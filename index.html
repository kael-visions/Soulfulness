Privacy Policy for Soulfulness

Effective Date: January 2026

Soulfulness is a mindfulness and spiritual wellness application designed to provide a calm, private space for reflection and personal growth. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Data Collection

Soulfulness does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data. The app does not require account creation, login, email submission, or any form of personal identification.

Local Data Storage

All journal entries, daily reflections, progress data, preferences, and mindful activity records are stored locally on the user’s device only. No data is uploaded to external servers or cloud storage services.

Uninstalling the app may permanently delete locally stored content. Users are responsible for backing up exported materials if desired.

Exporting or Sharing Content

Users may export journal entries as PDF files or share content using other applications installed on their device. These actions are initiated by the user and occur locally on the device. Soulfulness does not track, log, access, or store exported or shared content.

In-App Purchases

Soulfulness offers an optional one-time Premium purchase to unlock additional features. Payments are processed securely through Google Play Billing.

Soulfulness does not collect, process, or store payment or financial information. Payment processing is handled independently by Google under their own Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

The app relies on essential Google Play services for billing and distribution. Google may process limited technical data such as device identifiers as required for platform functionality. This processing is governed by Google’s own privacy policies.

Soulfulness does not independently access, store, or control this information.

Notifications

Optional local notifications may be used to provide reminders or enhance the user experience. Notification data is processed locally and is not transmitted externally. Users may disable notifications at any time through their device settings.

Data Security

Because Soulfulness operates primarily offline and does not transmit personal data, user privacy is protected by design. All stored data remains on the user's device.

Children’s Privacy

Soulfulness is intended for adults seeking mindfulness and personal reflection. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. As the app operates offline and without accounts, no personal data is collected from any users.

International Users

Soulfulness is distributed globally via Google Play. As no personal data is collected or processed by the developer, user privacy rights under applicable laws, including GDPR and similar regulations, are respected by design.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be reflected by updating the Effective Date shown above.
