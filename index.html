Privacy Policy for Soulfulness

Effective Date: January 2026

Soulfulness is a mindfulness and spiritual wellness application designed for personal reflection. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Data Collection

Soulfulness does not collect, transmit, or share personal or sensitive user data.

Data Storage

All journal entries, daily messages, game progress, and user preferences are stored locally on the user’s device only. No data is uploaded to external servers or cloud services.

Exporting or Sharing Content

When users choose to export or share content (such as PDF, TXT, JSON files, or via social applications), the action is performed entirely on the user’s device. Soulfulness does not track, log, or store exported or shared content.

Accounts and Authentication

Soulfulness does not require account creation, login, or user authentication.

Notifications

Optional local notifications may be used to provide reminders or enhance the user experience. Notification data is processed locally on the device and is not transmitted externally.

User Control

Users have full control over their data and may view or delete journal entries and saved content at any time within the app.

In-App Purchases

Soulfulness offers an optional one-time Premium purchase to unlock additional features. Payments are processed securely through Google Play Billing. Soulfulness does not collect, store, or have access to payment information.

Third-Party Services

Soulfulness uses standard services provided by Google Play, such as billing and essential platform services. These services may process limited technical information as required to operate. Soulfulness itself does not access, collect, or store this information.

Children’s Privacy

Soulfulness is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. As the app functions offline and without user accounts, no personal data is collected.
