Privacy Policy for Soulfulness

Soulfulness is a mindfulness and spiritual wellness app designed for personal reflection. This privacy policy explains how user data is handled.

Data Collection

Soulfulness does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or sensitive user data.

Data Storage

All journal entries, daily messages, game progress, and preferences are stored locally on the user’s device only. No data is uploaded to servers or cloud storage.

Exporting or Sharing

When you choose to export or share content (PDF, TXT, JSON, or via social apps), this action is performed entirely on your device. Soulfulness does not track, log, or store shared content.

Accounts & Login

Soulfulness does not require account creation, login, or authentication of any kind.

Notifications

Optional local notifications may be used to provide reminders or enhance the user experience. Notification data is processed locally on the device.

User Control

Users have full control over their data and can view or delete journal entries and saved content at any time within the app.

In-App Purchases

Soulfulness offers an optional one-time Premium purchase to unlock additional features. Payments are processed securely through Google Play. Soulfulness does not collect or store payment information.

Children’s Privacy

Soulfulness is intended for adult users and does not target children.
