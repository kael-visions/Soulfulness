Privacy Policy for Soulfulness

Soulfulness is a spiritual wellness app. This privacy policy explains how we handle user data.

Data Storage

All your journal entries, daily messages, and game progress are stored only on this device.

We do not collect, transmit, or share your data with any third parties.

You are responsible for the information stored on your device. Using device-level security, such as password, PIN, or biometric protection, is recommended.

Exporting or Sharing

When you export or share a journal entry (PDF, JSON, TXT, or via social apps), this happens entirely on your device; we do not track or log it.

Account & Login

No login, account creation, or cloud storage is required.

Optional Local Features

Optional local features, like notifications, are used only to improve your experience.

Control Over Your Data

You can view or delete your saved entries anytime within the app.

In-App Purchases

The app offers a one-time in-app purchase (“Premium”) to unlock all games and journal features. Payment is processed via Google Play and we do not store any payment information.
